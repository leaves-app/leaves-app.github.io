# Aica Developer Site

GitHub Pages site for Aica (ai-calendar).

## Pages

- `index.html` — Aica introduction and support contact
- `qa.html` — Frequently asked questions (search, category chips, `qa.html#<id>` opens a question)
- `faq.js` — Q&A open/close animation, search and hash linking
- `privacy.html` — Privacy policy
- `terms.html` — Terms of service
- `tokushoho.html` — Disclosure under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions
- `billing.html` — In-app purchase information (plans, auto-renewal, cancellation, refunds, restore)
- `assets/` — App icon and logo
- `app-ads.txt` — AdMob publisher declaration

## Source of truth

App behavior is defined in `~/Developments/ai-calendar/AICalendar/Specifications/product-specification.md`. Settings paths quoted in the Q&A follow the app's settings screen (アプリ設定: 外観／カレンダー／機能／通知, サポート, その他: ギャラリー／プレミアムプラン／表記・ライセンス).

## Before publishing

Confirm the actual data collected by the app and the purchase, subscription, cancellation, and refund details in the policy pages before offering the app or any paid features.

The Aica introduction area (screenshot placeholders) is left empty until app materials are provided.
