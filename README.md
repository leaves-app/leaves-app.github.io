# Aica Developer Site

GitHub Pages site for Aica (ai-calendar).

## Pages

- `index.html` — Aica introduction and support contact
- `privacy.html` — Privacy policy
- `terms.html` — Terms of service
- `tokushoho.html` — Disclosure under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions
- `billing.html` — In-app purchase information
- `app-ads.txt` — AdMob publisher declaration

## Before publishing

Replace the marked business-operator details in `tokushoho.html`. Confirm the actual data collected by the app and the purchase, subscription, cancellation, and refund details in the policy pages before offering the app or any paid features.
